Moving things. Drag a bar by its dots, the story feed by any line, and the time, the journal or this gear by its button. They snap to every edge of the window and to each other. Panels snapped together travel together, and an opened panel moves its neighbours aside until it folds.

The bars. Click a tool, or press 1–5 and 6–0. The grid button, or a double-click on a tool, shows every tool on that side. Drag a tool onto your bar to keep it, and off to take it out.

Work here. Click a spot on the land and your set rings it. Each click on a tool works that spot again. Turning or zooming the world closes the ring.
