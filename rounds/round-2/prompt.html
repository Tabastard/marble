Round 2 · The prompt

The first world in 3D

  • 12 Nov 2026
  • Claude Opus 5.5 in Claude Code
  • One-shot

The first round came out as a flat, side-on garden, so this prompt asks for full 3D and names the wrecking side for the first time.

The world

A small living landscape in full 3D, filling the lower part of a clear sphere with open sky above, that you orbit and zoom around.

Where the ground meets the sphere wall its underground layers show in cross-section, with fierce heat at the very bottom.

Two sets of tools

You shape it with two equal sets of tools, one for gentle tending and one for spectacular catastrophe, and whatever you do to it, it recovers in its own way.

Chains and discoveries

Its materials, weather and creatures each carry simple properties, so every action sets off chains of reactions, and the longest chains lead to rare discoveries.

Feel

It feels like a cheerful, snappy toy, light enough to run cool on a laptop, that you can leave running and keep poking at.

How to build it

Build first one thin slice where a single action triggers a visible chain through ground, water, heat and life, and make that slice delightful before adding anything.

Judge every addition by whether it makes the world respond in a new way.
