Tier 0 · Role

You are building a playable prototype from this brief.

Choose the technology yourself, ship one working slice, and let the brief's principles decide every trade-off.

Deliver something that runs, not a plan.

Tier 1 · The concept

A small living landscape sealed inside a transparent ball, with its underground layers visible at the edge, that you can nurture or disturb and that always recovers in its own way.

Its materials, weather and creatures each carry simple properties, so what you do sets off chains of reactions, and the longest chains lead to rare discoveries.

It feels like a cheerful, snappy toy you can leave running and keep poking at.

Build first a single thin slice where one action triggers a visible chain through ground, water and life, and make that slice delightful before adding anything.

Judge every addition by whether it makes the world respond in a new way.
