Round 5's prompt, word for word, given to a different model in an empty folder: no project notes, no earlier rounds, no way to ask a question and no browser to look at its own work.

The world

A small living landscape in full 3D, filling the lower part of a clear glass bubble that floats free in open space, with sky above the land, that you orbit and zoom around.

Where the ground meets the glass its underground layers show in cross-section, with fierce heat at the very bottom.

Reach

The whole landscape is in play, right out to the curved glass: every tool, cloud and creature can reach every part of it.

Two sets of tools

You shape it with two equal sets of tools, several in each.

One set tends: it sculpts the land itself, raising and lowering it, and brings water and new growth.

The other wrecks, with spectacular catastrophes that strike from below, from the sky and from the weather.

Disaster and recovery

Every disaster is an event with a beginning and an end: it flares, spends itself and leaves something new behind, and the tending tools are always strong enough to answer it.

Whatever you do to it, the world recovers in its own way.

Life arrives by itself wherever its needs are met, and each kind makes room for the next, so tending a place well is how richer creatures appear.

Chains and discoveries

Its materials, weather and creatures each carry simple properties, so every action sets off a chain of reactions and leaves an aftermath that stays until something changes it.

Any tool can be used on any aftermath, and that makes two kinds of discovery: small ones you simply witness the first time the world does something new, and rare ones you engineer by answering one aftermath with another tool, step by step, in the right order.

A rare discovery leaves behind something new that goes on acting in the world.

Feel

It feels like a cheerful, snappy toy, light enough to run cool on a laptop, that you can leave running and keep poking at.

Make each tool's reaction a delight to watch and hear.

Judge every addition by whether it makes the world respond in a new way.
